Redaction Gate — demo pages

SIH26171 · on-device visual perception for light-weight browser agents

Five minutes, in order. Each page is built to force one rung of the ladder, and the step line at the bottom of the panel says which rung answered and whether anything left the machine.

Tier 0 — the device answers

Every field labelled, every label distinct. The DOM already holds every fact the task needs.

4. Water board consumer servicestier 1 · open-ended

Tier 2 — one sealed artefact crosses the network

5. Scanned challantier 2 · sealed POST

A <canvas> scan and three icon buttons. There is no text node anywhere on the challan, so the DOM has nothing to say and the pixels have to answer.

describe what this challan is asking for

Open What was sent in the popup. Five findings, every one of them L3: the Aadhaar (ocr:verhoeff-ok), the PAN, the mobile, the name and the address — painted out before the frame was encoded. The invoice number two lines below the Aadhaar passes the Aadhaar checksum and is not painted, because the words beside it say what it is.

6. Uploaded enrolment sliptier 2 · sealed POST

The same argument with an uploaded <img> and an empty alt, which is how documents actually arrive. Name, date of birth, Aadhaar and address are all glyphs.

read the uploaded slip and tell me what it says

The enrolment number under the Aadhaar is a decoy: twelve digits, Verhoeff-valid, and left alone. Over-redaction on this page is about 7% of what was painted.

7. Beneficiary registrationtier 1 declines → tier 2

A page written by a bank for people who work at a bank. Bene. A/c No. (CBS) scores below the grammar's floor, so tier 1 is asked — and its answer is rejected.

fill account number with 50100234567

Step line: tier 1 refused: the local model put the same text into 4 fields, which is what it does instead of saying it does not know. The ladder does not accept a bad cheap answer; it pays for a better one.
