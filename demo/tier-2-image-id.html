Uploaded document

The applicant uploaded a photograph of their enrolment slip. Every identifier on it — the name, the date of birth, the Aadhaar, the address — is baked into the image. Nothing about it is in the DOM, so nothing about it can be redacted by reading the DOM.

enrolment-slip.png620 × 330uploaded 2 minutes ago

The alt attribute is empty on purpose, which is how uploaded documents arrive in real life. The enrolment number under the Aadhaar is a decoy: it is twelve digits and it passes the Aadhaar checksum, and it must survive the pass untouched because the words beside it say what it is.
